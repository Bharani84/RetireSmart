How Currency Rates Work
Click to learn about exchange rates
▾

Exchange Rate — How much 1 unit of a foreign currency equals in your retirement currency today. Example: 1 INR = 0.016 CAD.

Annual Change % — How much the foreign currency is expected to gain (+) or lose (−) value each year against your retirement currency. This compounds annually.

Example: If you hold 1,000,000 INR and the annual change is −2%, after 20 years those INR will be worth 32% less in CAD than today.

Tip: For emerging-market currencies (INR, MXN, BRL), −1% to −3%/yr is a reasonable conservative estimate. Developed-market currencies (USD, GBP, EUR) are closer to 0%.
