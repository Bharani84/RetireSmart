🚀 Multibagger Lab — finding the next 100×

Every big winner obeys one equation: stock return = EPS growth × P/E re‑rating. A 100× can be 30× earnings × 3× re‑rating, or 40× earnings × flat P/E, or a cheap unloved stock that simply gets re‑rated. The biggest winners (post‑2020 names like BSE, Gravita) fired both engines at once. This lab shows you the maths and the parameters to hunt for.
