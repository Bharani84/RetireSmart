🎯 The Golden Rule of Prepayment
Prepay in the first 5–7 years for maximum impact. In a 20-yr loan, ~70% of interest is paid in the first 8 years. ₹1L prepaid in Year 2 saves ~₹3L in interest. The same ₹1L in Year 15 saves only ~₹15K.
RBI rule: Floating-rate loans have ZERO prepayment penalty. Use it. Fixed-rate loans may charge 2–4%, so check before signing.
Reduce tenure, not EMI: When you prepay, ALWAYS ask the bank to keep EMI same and reduce tenure. They often default to "reduce EMI" which gives you less savings.
Prepay or invest? Rule of thumb: If post-tax loan rate > expected investment return, prepay. We'll compute this for your situation in Leverage Lab.

Call & Negotiate Rate Reset
Free · 30 minutes
Ask your bank to reset to current card rate. RBI mandates banks must offer this on RLLR/EBLR loans. They charge a small "conversion fee" (~₹1,000–10,000) but save you 25–50 bps. Email + branch visit + threaten to switch = often works.
💰 Potential save: 25–50 bps · ₹1–4 L lifetime
2
Switch from MCLR/Base to RLLR/EBLR
Free · 1 week
If your loan is still on the older MCLR or Base Rate regime, switch to Repo-Linked Lending Rate (RLLR). RBI rate cuts pass through in 3 months instead of being absorbed. Mandatory ask for any loan taken before Oct 2019.
💰 Potential save: 40–80 bps
3
Balance Transfer to Lower Bank
Effort: 2–4 weeks
If gap is ≥ 50 bps and remaining tenure > 7 years, transfer is worth it. SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Bajaj Housing aggressively poach. Get 3 written offers, then ask your current bank to match.
💰 Often saves 50–150 bps · ₹3–15 L lifetime
4
Improve CIBIL → Rate Reduction
3–6 months
A CIBIL score jump from 750 → 800+ qualifies you for the bank's premier "Vintage Customer" rate (often 25–40 bps lower). Pay all credit cards on time, keep utilization < 30%, don't apply for new credit for 6 months.
💰 Save: 25–40 bps
5
Switch to Female Co-Owner
Stamp duty rebate
Most banks offer 5–10 bps lower rate for loans in a woman's name. Bonus: 1–2% stamp duty rebate in Delhi, Maharashtra, UP, Rajasthan, Haryana when property is registered under a woman.
💰 Save: 5–10 bps rate + 1–2% stamp duty
6
Salary Account Bundling
Free
If your salary is credited to the same bank, ask for the "Salary Account Customer" concession. SBI, HDFC, ICICI offer 5–15 bps off. Also: government employees get 10–25 bps concession universally.
💰 Save: 5–25 bps
7
Pay 2 EMIs Upfront at Disbursal
One-time
Pre-pay first 2 EMIs from your down payment. Some banks (LIC HFL, Bajaj) reduce effective rate. Not universal — ask specifically. Net effect: ~10–20 bps reduction.
💰 Save: 10–20 bps
8
Festive / Year-End Offers
Timing matters
Banks slash rates aggressively in Sept–Nov (festive) and March (FY close) to hit targets. Time your application here. Free credit report + zero processing fees often bundled. Save 25–50 bps + ₹10–30K in fees.
💰 Save: 25–50 bps + fees

🚀 The 5 Smart Leverage Plays
🏗️
Under-Construction Arbitrage
15–25% lower price
Buy in RERA-registered Phase 1 launches at ~20% discount. Pay only pre-EMI (interest) during construction; full EMI starts on possession. By possession, market value is 25–40% higher. Risk: only RERA + Tier-1 builders.
💰 Capital gain: 25–40% over 3 yrs
🏘️
Let-Out 2nd Property
Unlimited 24(b)
2nd home, fully let out → full interest is deductible (no ₹2L cap, but losses capped at ₹2L/yr; rest carries forward 8 years). Rent yield 2.5–3.5% + tax-saved + appreciation = often 9–12% IRR.
💰 Net cost often < 3% after all benefits
💼
HRA + Home Loan Combo
Both deductions!
Work in Mumbai, own home in your hometown rented out → claim HRA exemption on Mumbai rent AND full 24(b) + 80C on home loan. Perfectly legal. Common for IT professionals. ₹3–5L additional deduction/yr.
💰 Save ₹1.5L+ tax / year
💎
Top-Up Home Loan vs Personal Loan
8% vs 14%
Need ₹10L for renovation/wedding/car? Top-up on existing home loan at ~8.5% beats personal loan at 12–18%. If used for house repair/extension, also deductible under 24(b) up to ₹30K/yr.
💰 Save 4–8% interest vs personal loan
⚖️
LAP (Loan Against Property)
9–11% rate
Already paid off some equity? LAP gives you 50–70% of current property value at 9–11% — cheaper than business loan or personal loan. Great for entrepreneurs deploying into business with 20%+ ROCE.
⚠️ Use only if return > 14% post-tax
📊
SIP During Loan, Prepay Lumpsum
Compound twice
If your post-tax loan rate is 6% and Nifty50 SIP yields 11% post-LTCG, SIP the extra cash for 5 years, then liquidate to prepay a chunk. Net gain: ~₹15–25L on a ₹1L/mo SIP over 5 years vs straight prepay.
💰 +₹15–25L over 5 yrs (typical)

🎯 The Realtor's Honest Take
Most Indians overweight emotional value of "owning". That's fine — it has real utility (no landlord, stability, freedom to renovate). But financially, in many metros today (Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad outskirts), rent + invest beats buy + EMI over 7-year horizons. Don't let "rent is throwing money away" guilt drive a multi-crore decision.
The break-even truth: Buying typically breaks even with renting around Year 7–9 for self-occupied homes at current rates (8.5%) and appreciation (7%). Staying < 5 years? Renting almost always wins. Staying > 12 years? Buying almost always wins.
The combo play: Rent where you work, buy where you'll retire. Rent a flat near the office (no EMI stress on prime real estate), buy a 2BHK in your hometown / tier-2 city you love (lower price, higher emotional return, often let-out for rental income).
